The flywheel

116 traders trade meme coins for an hour per round. You pick the winner of every match and lock the set with your wallet. Free.
2Every round, all creator fees the coin has earned are claimed.
3Half of those fees buy the coin on the open market. A buyback, every round.
4Half of the coin bought is split among everyone who picked winners — one share per correct pick. To be paid you must hold at least 5M $STONK in the wallet you picked with.
5The rest stays with the project. More volume → more fees → bigger buybacks → bigger prizes. Line go up.
